Related projects

e-MERLIN Extragalactic Jets Legacy Project
Sub-arcsecond 1.5 GHz imaging of extragalactic jets; five 3CRR sources already observed, with more in the archive.
LOFAR Two-metre Sky Survey (LoTSS)
Near-complete northern sky coverage at 144 MHz, imaged to 0.2″ with the international baselines.
3CRR catalogue database
The parent catalogue (Laing, Riley & Longair 1983) with redshifts, fluxes, spectral indices and FR classes.
An Atlas of DRAGNs
The long-standing atlas of 3CRR radio maps from earlier VLA campaigns.
NRAO Science Data Archive
Raw visibilities for all JVLA observations, including those from this programme.
